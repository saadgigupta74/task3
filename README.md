# Website Traffic Analysis

## Objective
Analyze website traffic data to understand user behavior.

## Key Metrics

### Page Views
The homepage and product pages received the highest number of views.

### Session Duration
Users spent the most time on product-related pages.
Mostly users are acquired via direct and organic searches and most of the revenue also is generated by these users.

### Drop-Off Points
A significant number of users exited after visiting the homepage.

### From the purchase journey 
it is seen that only  20% people go the product page from home page .

### Demographic details
Most revenue is generated from United States and Canada .
But the most number of active users are from India and United States and engagement time is highest in Canada and United States depicting high conversion rate.

## Insights
1. Product pages drive engagement.
2. Homepage has the highest exit count.
3. Users who visit multiple pages are more likely to convert.

## Recommendations
- Improve homepage CTA placement.
- Add related-product links.
- Reduce checkout friction.
